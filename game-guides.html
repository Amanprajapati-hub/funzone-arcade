Player Help Center

Game Guides & How to Play

New to a game? Explore these guides to understand the objective, basic gameplay, controls and scoring. All games are playable directly in your browser without downloads.

Action & Arcade Puzzle & Brain Strategy & Simulation Sports & Skill
Before you play: Controls can vary by game and device. When a game opens, check its on-screen instructions. Keyboard controls are available only where the game provides them; touch or pointer controls are used in supported games.

Action & Arcade Games

Racing

1. Cyber Dash

Objective: Survive a fast-paced futuristic highway run while avoiding traffic and collecting useful items.

How to play: Guide your runner through the course, watch for obstacles and collect fuel cells when it is safe. Use the available boost feature at useful moments.

Controls: Follow the keyboard or touch instructions shown when the game opens.

Tip: Look ahead rather than focusing only on the runner. React early when traffic appears.

Action

2. Galaxy Strike

Objective: Take on an arcade space-combat challenge and survive enemy encounters.

How to play: Control your spacecraft, pay attention to incoming threats and use the actions provided in the game to keep moving and attacking.

Controls: Check the in-game control instructions for the supported keyboard or touch actions.

Tip: Keep moving and watch the full play area so you can react to threats approaching from different directions.

Arcade

3. Neon Breaker

Objective: Play a neon-themed arcade challenge focused on breaking targets and keeping the action going.

How to play: Use the in-game controls to guide the paddle or game elements, keep track of the moving action and try to clear the targets.

Controls: Use the instructions displayed inside the game for your device.

Tip: Watch the movement carefully and position yourself early instead of waiting until the last moment.

Arcade

4. Neon Viper

Objective: Guide the neon snake through the play area and aim to improve your run.

How to play: Steer carefully, collect the items presented in the game and avoid collisions with obstacles or the snake itself.

Controls: Follow the displayed keyboard or touch controls.

Tip: Plan turns ahead and leave enough room to move safely as the snake grows.

Action

5. Meteor Strike

Objective: Survive an arcade challenge involving incoming space hazards.

How to play: Control your craft or player using the displayed controls, avoid incoming meteors and use the available actions to stay in the game.

Controls: Check the game's own instructions before starting.

Tip: Keep an eye on open spaces and avoid getting trapped near the edges.

Action

6. Cosmic Jetpack

Objective: Navigate a character through a space-themed challenge using jetpack movement.

How to play: Use the controls shown in the game to manage movement, avoid hazards and make progress through the level.

Controls: Refer to the on-screen instructions for keyboard or touch input.

Tip: Make controlled movements. Small adjustments can help you avoid hazards more reliably.

Racing

7. Drift Rush

Objective: Take on a drifting challenge and aim to complete your run while controlling the vehicle.

How to play: Steer through the course, manage your movement around turns and avoid losing control.

Controls: Use the controls displayed in the game. Supported touch controls are available on mobile.

Tip: Approach turns with control rather than making sudden corrections.

Skill

8. Balloon Pop Frenzy

Objective: Pop rising balloons before they escape from the screen.

How to play: Click or tap balloons as they rise. Normal balloons award 10 points and bonus balloons award 25 points. Each balloon that escapes costs one life. You start with five lives.

Controls: Click with a mouse or tap with a touchscreen.

Tip: Prioritize balloons that are close to escaping, and keep scanning the whole screen.

Arcade

9. Pinball Galaxy

Objective: Keep the pinball in play, hit bumpers and build your score.

How to play: Launch the ball using the launch area, then operate the left and right flippers when the ball approaches them. Bumper hits add points. You begin with three lives.

Controls: Use pointer or touch input in the lower play area. The left and right sides operate the corresponding flippers; the launch area is used to launch the ball.

Tip: Watch the ball's position and time your flipper actions as it approaches the lower part of the board.

Rhythm & Reflex

10. Rhythm Reactor

Objective: Hit falling notes in the correct lanes and maintain your energy during the 60-second challenge.

How to play: Watch the four lanes and press the matching input when a note reaches the target area. Accurate timing earns more points and builds your combo. Missed notes and mistimed presses reduce energy.

Controls: Keyboard A, S, D and F, or tap the matching lane.

Tip: Focus on the target line and use a steady rhythm. This is a timing challenge with generated notes, not music playback.

Puzzle & Brain Games

Puzzle

11. Jewel Fusion

Objective: Play a jewel-themed matching puzzle and create useful combinations.

How to play: Follow the board instructions and match or combine the pieces as directed by the game.

Controls: Use the displayed mouse or touch controls.

Tip: Check the available board before making a move and look for combinations that create space for future moves.

Memory

12. Memory Matrix

Objective: Test your memory by remembering and responding to the patterns shown in the game.

How to play: Pay attention to the displayed sequence or pattern, then repeat or select the matching elements as instructed.

Controls: Follow the on-screen mouse, touch or keyboard instructions.

Tip: Break longer patterns into smaller groups to make them easier to remember.

Number Puzzle

13. Sudoku Master

Objective: Complete a 9×9 Sudoku grid so every row, column and 3×3 box contains the digits 1–9 without repetition.

How to play: Select an empty cell and choose a number. You can also enter numbers with the keyboard. Use the difficulty options to choose a puzzle with a different number of empty cells. You have three hints and a maximum of five mistakes.

Controls: Click or tap a cell and a number button, or use keyboard digits 1–9. Arrow keys move the selection. Backspace, Delete or 0 clears an editable cell.

Scoring: Correct entries add points, wrong entries reduce points, and hints use points. A time bonus is available when you complete the puzzle.

Tip: Start with rows, columns or boxes that already contain many numbers.

Logic Puzzle

14. Nonogram Pixel Art

Objective: Reveal a hidden pixel picture by using the number clues along the rows and columns.

How to play: Each clue describes a run of consecutive filled cells. Use the Fill tool for cells you believe are part of the picture and the Mark tool to place an X on cells you believe should remain empty.

Controls: Tap or click cells and choose the Fill or Mark tool.

Scoring: Correct fills earn points. You have three hints; each hint uses points. The puzzle is complete when all required picture cells are filled.

Tip: Compare row and column clues together before filling uncertain cells.

Code Puzzle

15. Codebreaker

Objective: Discover a hidden four-digit code with no repeated digits.

How to play: Enter a four-digit guess. The feedback shows how many digits are in the correct position and how many are in the code but in a different position. Use the clues to narrow down the secret code.

Controls: Use the on-screen keypad or keyboard digits 0–9. Backspace edits your entry and Enter submits a guess.

Rules: Easy gives 10 attempts, Medium gives 8 and Hard gives 6. The code may begin with zero.

Tip: Keep track of digits you have already tested and avoid repeating the same information in later guesses.

Word Puzzle

16. Word Search Quest

Objective: Find the hidden words in an 8×8 letter grid.

How to play: Look at the word list, then select letters in a straight horizontal, vertical or diagonal line. Words can be selected forwards or backwards. Found words are marked and new words may be added to the list.

Controls: Click and drag with a mouse or touch and drag across the letters.

Scoring: Each correctly found word awards 100 points. Incorrect selections do not deduct points.

Tip: Search for unusual starting letters and scan diagonals as well as rows and columns.

Math Puzzle

17. Math 24 Challenge

Objective: Use the four numbers shown in a round and the available arithmetic operations to reach that round's target.

How to play: Combine the provided numbers using addition, subtraction, multiplication or division. Use each starting number once and work through the five rounds.

Controls: Select the numbers and operations using the game interface. Use the displayed undo or check controls when needed.

Scoring: A completed round awards 100 points, with an additional time bonus available.

Tip: Think about multiplication and division as well as addition. Check the target displayed for the current round.

Sliding Puzzle

18. Unblock Escape

Objective: Free the red target car by moving the other vehicles out of its path on a 6×6 board.

How to play: Horizontal vehicles move left or right, while vertical vehicles move up or down. Move the blocking vehicles strategically until the red car has a clear route to the exit on the right.

Controls: Select and drag a vehicle, use the arrow keys, or use the Back and Forward controls. Reset starts the puzzle again.

Scoring: The game starts at 500 points. Successful movement steps reduce the score, and a time bonus may be awarded for solving the puzzle.

Tip: Before moving a vehicle, check where it can move next and whether that will open space for another vehicle.

Logic Puzzle

19. Lights Out

Objective: Turn off every light on the 5×5 board.

How to play: Selecting a tile toggles that tile and its immediate neighbours above, below, left and right. Diagonal tiles are not affected. Choose a difficulty to generate a new solvable board.

Controls: Click or tap a tile. Arrow keys move the selection; Enter or Space toggles the selected tile.

Scoring: The score decreases with each move and with elapsed time. Completing the board can add a time bonus.

Tip: Remember that one move changes several lights at once. Think about how each move affects the surrounding tiles.

Connection Puzzle

20. Circuit Connect

Objective: Connect the source (S) to the target (T) by creating a continuous electrical path.

How to play: Rotate tiles clockwise to line up their connections. The path must connect through matching openings until the source can reach the target.

Controls: Click or tap a tile to rotate it. Arrow keys move the selection; Enter or Space rotates the selected tile.

Scoring: The score is affected by the number of moves and elapsed time.

Tip: Trace a possible route from S to T and rotate tiles along that route before changing unrelated pieces.

Shape Puzzle

21. Tangram Builder

Objective: Arrange seven geometric pieces to match the target outlines.

How to play: Drag each piece from the tray towards its matching target. Rotate a selected piece until its orientation matches the outline. A correctly positioned piece snaps into place.

Controls: Click or tap and drag pieces. Use the Rotate button or R/Enter key to rotate the selected piece. Escape deselects it.

Scoring: Correctly placed pieces add points. A time bonus is available when all seven pieces are placed.

Tip: Compare both the shape and orientation of a piece before placing it.

Strategy & Simulation Games

Tower Defense

22. Mini Tower Defense

Objective: Protect your base by building towers that stop enemies from reaching it.

How to play: Tap an available build pad to place a tower. Select an existing tower to upgrade it. Start a wave when you are ready. Towers automatically target enemies within range.

Controls: Tap or click build pads and towers. Use the Upgrade, Start Wave and Reset buttons.

Starting resources: You begin with 120 coins and 10 lives. Building a tower costs 50 coins; upgrades have a separate cost shown in the game.

Tip: Place towers where they can cover useful sections of the enemy route. Upgrade towers when it helps your defence.

Board Strategy

23. Reversi Arena

Objective: Finish the board with more disks than the computer opponent.

How to play: Place a disk on a legal empty square to capture one or more opponent disks. A capture happens when an opponent line is bounded by your disks. Captures can happen horizontally, vertically and diagonally.

Controls: Click or tap a legal square. The computer takes its turn automatically.

Rules: If a player has no legal move, that turn is skipped. The game ends when neither side can move.

Tip: Think carefully before taking a corner or edge-adjacent square; each move can change several lines on the board.

Tactical Strategy

24. Kingdom Tactics

Objective: Defeat the opposing king while protecting your own king.

How to play: Select one of your units, then choose a highlighted legal destination or attack. Each turn allows one action. Your army includes a king, soldiers, a knight and an archer, each with different movement or attack rules.

Controls: Tap or click one of your units, then select a highlighted square. The computer responds automatically after its turn.

Rules: The king and soldiers can move or attack adjacent squares. The knight moves in an L-shape to an empty square. The archer attacks along a clear horizontal or vertical line within its range.

Tip: Protect your king and consider the range of enemy units before choosing a move.

Management

25. Factory Manager

Objective: Manage production, earn coins and sell at least 120 goods.

How to play: Your factory produces goods automatically. Use Quick Produce for an immediate production boost, sell available goods to earn coins and purchase upgrades for your machine, production speed or selling value.

Controls: Use the on-screen buttons to produce, sell and purchase upgrades. The game supports touch and pointer input.

Tip: Choose upgrades based on what you need most: faster automatic production, more goods from Quick Produce or a higher selling price.

Sports, Skill & Precision Games

Sports

26. Mini Golf Worlds

Objective: Complete all five golf courses by guiding the ball into each cup.

How to play: When the ball is still, drag from near it to set the shot direction and power. Release to hit the ball. The launch direction is opposite to the direction of your drag.

Controls: Click or touch near the ball, drag to aim and set power, then release to shoot.

Scoring: Each shot counts as a stroke. Your displayed score is based on the strokes taken on completed holes.

Tip: Use shorter, controlled shots near obstacles and the cup. The ball must be moving slowly enough when it reaches the cup to finish the hole.

Physics Puzzle

27. Rope Rescue

Objective: Cut the ropes in a way that helps the character reach the rescue target.

How to play: Swipe across an active rope or tap close to it to cut it. The character responds to gravity and movement as the ropes are removed. Complete the level by reaching the target.

Controls: Use mouse or touch input to swipe across a rope or tap near a rope segment.

Rules: The game contains five levels. If the character falls out of the play area, retry the level.

Tip: Consider how the character will swing or fall after each cut. Cutting every rope immediately may not produce the best route.

Laser Puzzle

28. Laser Bounce

Objective: Guide the laser beam from its emitter to the target in each level.

How to play: Click or tap the blue mirrors to rotate them. Watch the beam path after each rotation and try to direct it around obstacles towards the glowing target.

Controls: Click or tap mirrors to change their orientation. Select Next Level after activating the target.

Scoring: Mirror rotations count as moves and affect your score.

Tip: Trace the beam's route from the emitter and identify which mirror needs to redirect it next.

Precision

29. Precision Archery

Objective: Aim carefully and score as many points as possible across ten arrows and ten rounds.

How to play: Aim towards the target and shoot. The target moves vertically in later rounds, and changing wind can affect the arrow's path. Wait for the previous arrow to finish its flight before taking the next shot.

Controls: Move or drag to aim, click or tap to shoot. Arrow keys adjust aim and Space shoots.

Scoring: Points depend on how close the arrow passes to the target centre. The final rating is based on your total score.

Tip: Check the wind indicator and target position before each shot. The aiming guide is approximate, so allow for the arrow's flight path.

Skill & Arcade

30. Tower Stack

Objective: Build a tall tower by stacking moving blocks as accurately as possible.

How to play: Follow the game's on-screen prompts to place each moving block on the tower. Try to keep the blocks aligned as the tower grows.

Controls: Use the controls displayed inside the game. Follow the instructions for your device.

Tip: Watch the moving block carefully and time each placement to keep as much of the tower aligned as possible.

Need more help? Return to the arcade and open a game to see its instructions and available controls. Game rules and difficulty options may differ from one title to another.
