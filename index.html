ABOUT FUNZONE ARCADE

FunZone Arcade is a browser-based gaming destination featuring 30 mini games across puzzle, action, racing, strategy, sports and other genres. Our goal is to make casual gaming easy to access, with games that can be opened directly in a web browser without installing separate applications.

🎮

30 Browser Mini Games

Explore 30 games covering different styles of play, including arcade action, racing challenges, number puzzles, word games, board strategy, simulation and precision-based challenges. There is a variety of gameplay to explore.

🧩

Different Ways to Play

Choose a game based on your interests. Solve logic puzzles, test your memory, manage a virtual factory, defend a base, guide a golf ball or try an arcade reflex challenge.

⚡

No Separate Downloads

Games run directly through your supported web browser. You do not need to install a separate game application to explore the FunZone Arcade collection.

📱

Desktop & Mobile Gaming

Access the arcade on supported desktop computers, tablets and smartphones. Many games support touch or pointer input, while keyboard controls are available in selected games. Controls depend on the individual game.

🏆

Personal High Scores

Supported games can save your personal best scores in your browser. Revisit a game, compare your new result with your previous best and try to improve your performance.

🔊

Browser Audio Experience

Selected games include browser-based sound effects and audio elements. Audio availability and gameplay features vary depending on the game.

🕹️

How to Start Playing

Browse the game collection, use the category filters or search for a title. Select a game to open it and read its on-screen instructions before starting.

📖

Game Guides & Instructions

Need help understanding a game? Visit our Game Guides page for information about objectives, rules, controls and gameplay tips for the arcade collection. Explore Game Guides.

Privacy & Website Information
