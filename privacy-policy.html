Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to FunZone Arcade. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit or use our website, how it is used, and what choices may be available to you.

2. Information We Collect

You can play games on FunZone Arcade without creating an account or providing personal information.

However, certain information may be processed when you use our website:

  • Technical information: Our hosting provider and other website service providers may automatically process information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and general usage data.
  • Contact form information: If you contact us through our website, you may provide your name, email address, topic and message. We use this information to respond to your inquiry.
  • Browser-stored information: Our website may store game-related preferences, such as favorites and high scores, in your browser's local storage.

3. Contact Form and Formspree

Our contact form uses Formspree, a third-party form processing service. When you submit the form, the information you provide, including your name, email address, topic and message, is sent to Formspree for processing and delivery to us.

Formspree may process information according to its own privacy policy and service terms. Please review its privacy information before submitting personal information.

We use submitted information to respond to messages and inquiries. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information through our contact form.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

FunZone Arcade may use browser local storage to remember certain game preferences, including favorites and high scores. This information is stored in your browser and helps provide a more convenient gaming experience.

Our website and third-party services may also use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, advertising and measurement.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some services.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

FunZone Arcade may use Google AdSense and other advertising services to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalize, limit, measure and report advertisements, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and privacy laws.

Depending on your location and available choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized. You may also be able to manage advertising personalization through Google's settings.

Learn more about Google's advertising practices:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings

6. Third-Party Services and External Links

Our website may rely on third-party providers for hosting, contact form processing and advertising. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Our website may also contain links to external websites. We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

7. Children's Privacy

FunZone Arcade is intended for a general audience. We do not require visitors to create accounts or provide personal information to play games.

Our contact form should not be used by children to submit personal information without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our contact form, please contact us so that we can review the request and take appropriate action.

8. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information submitted through our contact form may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain relevant records or meet applicable legal obligations. Third-party providers may have their own retention practices.

9. Your Privacy Choices

You may be able to:

  • Manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.
  • Clear locally stored website data through your browser settings.
  • Manage Google's advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings.
  • Contact us with questions or requests concerning information you submitted through our contact form.

Available privacy rights and choices may depend on your location and applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or information submitted through our contact form, please visit the Contact section on our FunZone Arcade homepage.
